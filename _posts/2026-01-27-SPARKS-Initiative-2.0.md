SPARKS Initiative 2.0 is a workshop that is conducted weekly starting on the 27th January 2026 to 31th of March.

This workshop aims to sharpen participants’ technical skills by teaching them applicable skills that can be utilized throughout their university journey. The workshop will have around 10 groups, with each group being divided into 2 people per group.
