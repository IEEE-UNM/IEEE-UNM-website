Circuits 101 is a hands-on workshop particularly designed for Year One and Foundation students to explore the fundamentals of electrical and electronic engineering. Participants will begin by learning essential breadboard circuit concepts, including series and parallel circuit connections, and will practice building simple component circuits in pairs. 

After mastering the basics, participants will explore common electronic components and be guided through two exciting circuit projects. The first challenge is building a water level indicator circuit, allocated about 35 minutes. The second challenge is constructing a clap switch circuit, with 40 minutes provided. Both activities will be conducted in groups of four to encourage peer discussion and collaborative learning. Throughout the process, the committee will provide close guidance to ensure safety.
