In the second event of a 3-part internship series event in collaboration with Career Advisory Service (CAS), Chapter 2 - Craft a Winning Resume consists of two main sessions: a resume writing workshop and a resume competition. A CAS representative will lead the workshop, providing practical guidance and tips for crafting effective resumes. For the competition, participants will receive a standardized virtual character profile as a template, ensuring fairness while allowing students to apply their skills in creating a professional and tailored resume.
