The STM32 Workshop aims to introduce participants to the fundamentals of embedded systems and microcontroller programming using the STM32 platform. Through interactive sessions, participants will learn to configure GPIOs, timers, and interrupts using STM32CubeIDE. 

The workshop includes a hands-on activity where participants design and implement a simple traffic light system with pedestrian control, reinforcing their understanding of hardware-software integration. By the end of the session, students will gain valuable experience in programming microcontrollers and applying their knowledge to real-world applications in automation and control systems.
