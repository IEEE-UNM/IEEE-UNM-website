Women in STEM 2026 is a collaborative, student-led initiative that enhances the UNM student experience through inclusive, interdisciplinary activities beyond the classroom. 

The programme supports leadership and personal development by involving students in event planning, teamwork, and cross-society collaboration. 
Through charity initiatives and outreach to pre-university students, it promotes social responsibility and meaningful community engagement. 
By celebrating women’s contributions in STEM and fostering collaboration across societies, the initiative creates lasting value for an inclusive, supportive, and engaged campus community.
