In the first event of a 3-part internship series event, Chapter 1 - Spill Your Tea is a peer-to-peer sharing event, featuring three student speakers who have internship experience at different companies. Through honest reflection and real-life experiences, the speakers will share insights on internship expectations, workplace culture, application tips and key lessons learned to help students better prepare for their own internship journey.
