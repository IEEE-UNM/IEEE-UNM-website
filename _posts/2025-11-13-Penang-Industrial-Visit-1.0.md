This visit is a two day and one night program to Penang, designed to expose participants to leading semiconductor and technology companies. On the first day, participants visits Plexus Riverside in Bayan Lepas, followed by a networking session in the evening. Then arriving at Eureka Hotel Penang for the night.  

On the second day, participants visits Vitrox Technologies in Batu Kawan to learn about automated vision inspection systems, followed by SanDisk Storage Malaysia to gain insights into advanced data storage solutions.
